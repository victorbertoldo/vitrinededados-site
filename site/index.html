Sounds familiar?

Your company has plenty of data. What it’s missing is a design.

This is rarely a tooling problem. You can buy the best warehouse on the market and still get five answers to the same question, because nobody modeled what the question means.

Same word, five numbers

“Revenue” in finance isn’t “revenue” in sales. Every dashboard has its own version, and every meeting starts with an argument about which one is right.

Pipelines held together with duct tape

Scripts nobody owns, jobs that fail silently, and a weekly ritual of fixing last week’s loads before anyone notices.

History that quietly changes

A customer moves region or a product changes category, and last year’s reports change with it. Nothing is reproducible, nothing is auditable.

A platform that grew by accident

A warehouse here, a lake there, a few tools on top. The cloud bill keeps growing and nobody can draw the whole picture on one page.

Tools don’t fix this. Architecture does. And no, AI won’t fix it either: AI can’t fix chaos, it needs structure.

Our approach

Let’s be honest: most data projects start with a tool and end with a mess. We start at the other end.

First, how your business works and what it needs to know. Then the model that makes each answer true everywhere: one customer, one order, one revenue, across every system and every report. Only then the tools and the pipelines.

The model is the product

Real data warehousing, modeled across your whole company.

We use proven modeling techniques, picked for the problem rather than for fashion, and often combined in layers.

Dimensional modeling

Star schemas with conformed dimensions, so finance, sales and operations slice the same facts by the same customer, product and calendar.

Best for

BI, self-service analytics and metrics people can trust.

Data Vault

Hubs, links and satellites that integrate many sources, absorb change without rework and keep the complete, auditable history of your business.

Best for

Enterprise integration layers, many changing sources, audit and compliance.

Lakehouse architecture

Layered bronze, silver and gold zones on open table formats such as Apache Iceberg, mixing warehouse discipline with lake-scale storage.

Best for

Large or semi-structured data, ML and AI workloads, avoiding lock-in.

FAQ

The things people ask us first.

Still have a question? Bring it to a first conversation.

Do you work with companies in the United States?

Yes. We work remotely with companies in the US and elsewhere, in English. Brasília time (UTC−3) overlaps most of the US business day, so live working sessions are easy to schedule. We can engage on a project basis or as an independent contractor embedded in your team.

Are you tied to a specific vendor or tool?

No. We are tool-agnostic and have no reseller agreements. We recommend Snowflake, Databricks, Dremio, a fully open-source stack or what you already have, based on your data, your team and your budget.

Dimensional model or Data Vault: which one do we need?

Often both, in different layers. Data Vault is great for integrating many changing sources with full history; dimensional models are what analysts and BI tools should read. We decide based on your sources, your audit needs and who consumes the data, and we explain the trade-offs in plain language.

We already have a data platform. Can you help without starting over?

Most of our work starts exactly there. We assess what exists, keep what works, and fix the model, the pipelines or the costs where it matters most. Rewrites happen only when they clearly pay off.

Who will actually do the work?

A senior data architect, directly. You are not handed over to a junior team after the sales call. When a project needs extra hands, we tell you upfront who joins and why.

How do engagements and pricing work?

It depends on the shape of the work: a fixed-scope project, a monthly advisory retainer, or hourly contract work embedded in your team. After a first conversation we send a written proposal with scope, timeline and price.

Do you also work with AI?

Yes, from the data side. AI can't fix chaos: it needs structure. We build the governed, well-modeled data that AI assistants, retrieval systems and agents depend on, and we design how they access it safely.
