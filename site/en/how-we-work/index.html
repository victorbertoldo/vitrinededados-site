How we work

A consulting framework, not a sales funnel.

Five steps that keep every engagement clear and predictable, flexible enough to start wherever you are today. Some clients need all five; others only need a second opinion on step two.

Engagement steps

  1. Discover & assess

    We listen first. We interview the people who use the data, map sources, models, pipelines and costs, and find the questions the business still can’t answer with confidence.

    • Current-state diagnosis
    • Pain points ranked by business impact
    • Quick wins you can act on right away

  2. Design the architecture

    We design the target architecture and the data model together with your team, and write down every important decision with its trade-offs. No black boxes.

    • Target architecture and data model
    • Architecture decision records
    • Roadmap with effort, risk and sequence

  3. Implement & integrate

    We build in small, useful increments: the first subject area in production early, then the next. Models, pipelines and tests are code, versioned and reviewed.

    • Models and pipelines in production
    • Automated tests and orchestration
    • Regular demos with the business

  4. Validate & optimize

    We reconcile numbers with the business until they match, then tune performance and cost so the platform stays fast and affordable as it grows.

    • Reconciliation with source systems
    • Data quality monitoring
    • Performance and cost tuning

  5. Transfer knowledge & support

    Your team should own what we build. We document, train and mentor, and stay available for ongoing support and evolution if you want us to.

    • Documentation and runbooks
    • Hands-on training for your team
    • Optional ongoing support
