About Vitridata

Modern data deserves better than duct tape.

We’re on a mission to end metric arguments, fragile pipelines and platforms nobody can explain. We design and build data architectures that model how your business actually works, so every team, report and AI tool reads from the same truth.

Who we are

A specialist boutique, not an outsourcing factory.

Vitridata started in Brazil as Vitrine de Dados. After years building data platforms, our founder kept seeing the same pattern: companies bought the tools, hired the people, and still argued about the numbers, because nobody had designed the model underneath.

So that became our job. No 500-person delivery center and no junior bench learning on your project. Every engagement is led by a senior data architect who designs the solution and stays hands-on through delivery, backed by our team of data engineers, analytics engineers and cloud and DevOps engineers. We work from Brazil with clients in Brazil, the United States and beyond.

What we think

Let’s be honest.

Most data platforms are built tool-first, by people who already know the tools, for problems nobody fully described. We build for companies that need answers they can trust, and for the teams who have to live with the platform afterwards.

We believe:

  • 01
    AI can’t fix chaos. It needs structure.And structure is a design decision. Models, definitions and history come first; the clever part comes after.
  • 02
    “Revenue” shouldn’t mean five different things.Not depending on the dashboard, not depending on who built the report. One definition, modeled once, used everywhere.
  • 03
    Solving business problems matters more than writing code.The best pipeline is sometimes the one you didn’t need to build. We measure success in answers people trust, not in lines of SQL.
  • 04
    Collaboration shouldn’t require translation.Engineers and business teams should read the same model, in the same words, on the same page.
  • 05
    Architecture is for the long run.If your team can’t understand, maintain and extend it two years from now, it isn’t finished. That is why knowledge transfer is part of every engagement.
